How it works

Add it. Watch it. Catch the drop.

Add a product to your watchlist

Paste a product URL, search by keyword, or scan a barcode with your camera. PriceDrop resolves it through the TwoBits Worker, fills in the current price, and backfills its price history from Rainforest.

It checks prices in the background

A WorkManager job refreshes price and coupons for every active product on your schedule (every 24 hours by default) — honoring Wi-Fi-only, charging-only, and check-frequency settings, with quiet hours from 22:00 to 08:00.

Get alerted at the right moment

When the price crosses your target or drops past your alert threshold, PriceDrop posts a notification. Tap it to open the product, compare other sellers, and copy any valid coupon.

Two ways to power it

BYOK or Pro

Like every twobits app, PriceDrop never locks you in. Run it on your own provider keys, or subscribe to PriceDrop Pro for fully managed data.

Bring your own keys

Add your own OpenAI, search, shopping, Rainforest, or coupon keys. In BYOK mode, calls go directly to each upstream provider — the twobits Worker is never involved. You pay each provider directly.

PriceDrop Pro

Monthly or annual via Google Play. Uses an internally-managed model and routes through the TwoBits Worker — no keys to manage. Validated by RevenueCat (pricedrop_pro entitlement).

About this app

PriceDrop watches the price of everything you want to buy and tells you the moment it's worth buying. Add a product three ways — paste a URL, search by keyword, or scan its barcode. PriceDrop resolves it, fills in the current price, and backfills its full price history so you can see whether today's price is actually a good deal. Then it works in the background. A scheduled job refreshes prices and hunts for coupons on your terms — Wi-Fi-only, charging-only, on your chosen frequency, and silenced during quiet hours (22:00–08:00). When a price hits your target or drops past your alert threshold, you get a notification. What you get: • Price-history chart — observed price, effective price (incl. shipping & fees), and your target line, across 7D / 30D / 90D / All • Other sellers — competing offers sorted by effective price, not just the headline number • Coupons — with Valid / Untested / Restricted / Expired badges and one-tap copy • Drops feed — price drops, coupons, and new historical lows across your watchlist • Activity timeline — added, checked, dropped, coupon found, alert sent • Ask assistant — a shopping-scoped AI for buying questions • Barcode scanner — resolve a UPC and track it in seconds Per-provider AI config gives you an Off / BYOK / Pro toggle for OpenAI, web search, shopping search, Rainforest price tracking, and coupons. Bring your own keys and pay each provider directly, or subscribe to PriceDrop Pro for fully managed data.
