Your data stays yours

twobits products are tools, not platforms. We don't run cloud services, we don't have user accounts, and we don't collect data. Here's exactly how each product handles your information.

Core principles

vpn_key

Bring your own key

AI processing uses your API key, billed directly to your provider at their published rates. Your key is stored on-device in Android's local DataStore. We never see it server-side.

workspace_premium

Pro managed key

Pro subscriptions (Scrybe Pro at $1.99/mo, plus Shelf-Snap Pro and PriceDrop Pro) route calls through the TwoBits Worker (api.twobits.app). The worker validates your RevenueCat entitlement, then forwards to the upstream provider. The provider key stays server-side — never on your device.

smartphone

On-device AI

Scrybe offers fully local processing: Whisper tiny/base/small for transcription (via Sherpa-ONNX), Gemma 2 2B for transforms (via MediaPipe). In local mode, zero data leaves your device.

storage

Local-first storage

All user data — recordings, photos, transcripts, inventory, and your price watchlist — is stored on-device in Room databases and app-private file storage. We have no cloud backend, no sync service, no user database.

visibility_off

No telemetry

No Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Sentry, no Crashlytics. The only external reporting is anonymous crash reports via Google Play Console. We don't know who you are.

delete_outline

Delete means delete

When you delete a recording, photo, or item from the app, it's gone from the device filesystem immediately. There's no cloud backup to purge. Uninstalling removes everything.

Pro subscription architecture

How managed API access works

Pro subscribers across all three apps don't need their own provider keys. Instead, calls route through the TwoBits Worker at api.twobits.app. The worker validates the subscription via RevenueCat, then proxies the request to the upstream provider (OpenAI, search, shopping data) using a server-side key. Your content goes directly to that provider — the worker does not log, store, or inspect it.

Scrybe

Voice to Document — data handling details

Data typeWhere it goesRetention
Audio recordings On-device Stored in app-private storage. Sent to your transcription provider only when you trigger transcription (or auto-transcribe is on). Until you delete it. No auto-expiry unless you configure one.
Transcripts & transforms On-device Generated text stored in Room database. The AI provider may retain prompts per their own policy. Until you delete the session.
Tasks On-device Extracted action items stored in session_tasks table (Room DB). Assignee, due date, and status are local-only. Until you delete the session or individual task.
Audio sent to AI Your provider Via HTTPS to OpenAI Whisper (BYOK) or via api.twobits.app (Pro). Long recordings are chunked (≤16 min per chunk). OpenAI: 30 days by default (opt out in dashboard). Pro proxy: zero retention.
On-device AI On-device Whisper tiny/base/small via Sherpa-ONNX. Gemma 2 2B via MediaPipe. Models downloaded once, stored locally. Models persist until you delete them in Settings.
API keys On-device Stored in Android DataStore. Pro users send a RevenueCat App User ID instead — no API key on device. Until you remove them or uninstall.
Location data On-device Optional — coarse GPS captured at recording start, reverse-geocoded to "City, State." Requires explicit permission grant. Until you delete the session or disable the setting.
Speaker profiles On-device Named person profiles and speaker assignments stored in Room. Used for diarization labels. Until you delete them.
Crash reports Play Console Anonymous stack traces via Google Play. No user identifiers. Per Google Play Console retention.

Third-party services

What we connect to — and only when you ask

OpenAI (Whisper / GPT / Vision)

Transcription and transforms (Scrybe), item analysis and price research (Shelf-Snap), and the shopping assistant (PriceDrop). Audio, text, or images sent directly using your key — or via api.twobits.app for Pro users.

schedule Only when you trigger an AI action

RevenueCat

Manages Pro subscriptions across all three apps. The app sends an anonymous App User ID to verify entitlement (e.g. pricedrop_pro). RevenueCat receives no recording, transcript, inventory, or watchlist data.

schedule On app launch (subscription check) and when purchasing Pro

Jina AI, Brave Search, SearchAPI.io & Serper.dev

Web and shopping search for Shelf-Snap market research and PriceDrop offer discovery. Text queries based on item or product attributes are sent to your chosen provider — either directly (BYOK) or via the TwoBits Worker (Pro).

schedule Only when you research a price or refresh a watched product

Rainforest & Couponlayer

PriceDrop only. Rainforest supplies product data, competing-seller offers, price-history backfill, and barcode resolution. Couponlayer discovers and verifies coupon codes. Both are reached through the TwoBits Worker (Pro) or your own keys (BYOK).

schedule When you add a product or a background check runs

Google Play Console

Android's built-in crash reporting. Sends anonymous stack traces when the app crashes. No user identifiers, no usage analytics, no content data.

schedule Automatic on crash — no user data included

Common questions

Do you have access to my recordings or photos? expand_more
No. We have no cloud backend, no sync service, and no way to access data on your device. When you use a cloud AI provider, your data goes directly from your device to the provider's API — we are not in the middle. Pro users' data passes through our Cloudflare worker but is not logged, stored, or inspected.
How does Pro work without my own API key? expand_more
Pro subscribers' AI calls route through a Cloudflare Worker at api.twobits.app. The worker validates your subscription via RevenueCat, then proxies the request to the upstream provider using a server-side API key. Your audio, photos, or text go to that provider — we proxy it without logging or storing any content. The provider key never touches your device. Scrybe Pro also enforces a monthly spend cap server-side.
What happens in on-device mode? expand_more
Nothing leaves your device. Whisper runs locally via Sherpa-ONNX for transcription, and Gemma 2 2B runs via MediaPipe for transforms. No internet connection is needed or used. Models are downloaded once and stored on your phone.
Does OpenAI keep my data? expand_more
OpenAI retains API data for 30 days by default, but you can opt out in your OpenAI account settings. When using Pro tiers, the same OpenAI retention policy applies to the server-side key. We recommend reviewing OpenAI's data usage policy for API users.
What does Shelf-Snap send to web search providers? expand_more
When you tap "Research price," Shelf-Snap sends a search query based on the item's category, brand, and description to your chosen search provider (Jina AI, Brave Search, SearchAPI.io, or Serper.dev). The search results are then sent to OpenAI for pricing analysis. No photos are sent to search providers — only text queries.
Do you use any analytics or tracking SDKs? expand_more
No. No Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Sentry, no Crashlytics. The only external reporting is Google Play Console's built-in crash reporting, which sends anonymous stack traces with no user identifiers.
How do I delete all my data? expand_more
Uninstall the app. All data is stored on-device in app-private storage — uninstalling removes recordings, photos, transcripts, inventory items, API keys, and settings. There is no cloud account to deactivate. For data already sent to AI providers, check their respective retention policies.
What does PriceDrop send off my device? expand_more
Only what's needed to look up a product: search terms, URLs, or scanned barcodes go to the TwoBits Worker, which fetches product and offer data, price history, and barcode resolution from Rainforest, and coupons from Couponlayer. Your watchlist itself stays in a local database on your phone — it is never uploaded. Background price checks respect your Wi-Fi-only, charging-only, frequency, and quiet-hours settings.
Is the code open source? expand_more
The apps live at github.com/punassuming/twobits, GPLv3 licensed — you can read every line of code and verify every network call. The monorepo includes all three Android apps and shared modules (billing, network, design). The TwoBits Worker that powers Pro subscriptions lives in a separate repository, github.com/punassuming/twobits-worker.
