Our commitment

Bring your own key

AI processing uses your API key, billed directly to your provider. Or subscribe to Pro and we handle it — routed through the TwoBits Worker so the key never touches your device.

Local-first storage

Scrybe runs Whisper and Gemma on-device. Shelf-Snap and PriceDrop keep everything in a local Room database. No cloud backend, no sync service, no accounts.

No analytics SDKs. No ad networks. No telemetry beyond anonymous Play Console crash reports. We don't know who you are.

About twobits

A small workshop.
Three good tools.

twobits is an independent software studio. All three apps live in a single monorepo and share a design system (DM Sans, Material 3 tokens), a billing module (RevenueCat), and the TwoBits Worker proxy for Pro subscribers — but each is independently buildable and independently useful.

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Hilt, Room. minSdk 26, targetSdk 35. GPLv3 licensed. You can read every line of code and verify every network call.
