How it works

Snap. Analyze. List or donate.

Capture one or more photos

Open the camera and photograph any item — clothing, electronics, furniture, books, anything. CameraX captures multiple angles; thumbnails appear in a scrollable strip. Framing grid and flash toggle built in.

AI drafts the listing

Photos go to OpenAI Vision. The API returns category, description, condition, estimated value, and a confidence score — saved as a draft record you can review and edit.

Price, list, or donate

The Market tab researches resale value with cited sources. The List tab lets you cross-list to selling platforms at platform-adjusted prices. Or export a donation summary with one-tap CSV.

What's inside

photo_camera

Multi-photo capture

CameraX with framing grid, reticle, capture flash, and dynamic hint text. Numbered photo strip shows all angles. Remove individual shots before analysis.

auto_awesome

AI item analysis

OpenAI Vision returns structured JSON: category, description, condition, estimated value, and confidence. Values are always labeled as estimates — never presented as authoritative.

price_change

Market research

The Market tab queries a fast OpenAI model plus an optional web search (Jina, Brave, SearchAPI.io, or Serper). It surfaces average sold price, a range, per-platform suggestions, and comparable listings with cited sources.

storefront

Cross-listing

Select selling platforms — eBay, Mercari, OfferUp, FB Marketplace, Craigslist — and record listings at platform-adjusted prices. Listed/Sold status badges on every inventory card.

volunteer_activism

Donation summary

A summary screen shows per-item subtotals and the total estimated donation value. One-tap CSV export creates a file in device storage, shareable via the system chooser.

manage_search

Inventory management

Search by category, brand, or description. Filter chips: All / Listed / Unlisted / Drafts. Category-specific icon fallbacks. Photo thumbnails on every row.

About this app

Shelf-Snap helps you document, value, and export a donation or resale inventory using your camera and AI. Snap photos of any item — clothing, electronics, furniture, books, kitchenware. OpenAI Vision analyzes each photo and returns a structured draft: category, description, condition, estimated value, and confidence score. All values are clearly labeled as estimates. Use the Market tab to research real resale prices. Shelf-Snap queries a fast OpenAI model plus an optional web search (Jina AI, Brave, SearchAPI.io, or Serper) and surfaces average sold prices, price ranges, per-platform suggestions, and comparable listings with cited sources. Ready to sell? The List tab lets you select platforms — eBay, Mercari, OfferUp, FB Marketplace, Craigslist — and record listings at platform-adjusted prices. Track Listed and Sold status from your inventory. Donating instead? The summary screen shows per-item subtotals and total estimated donation value. One-tap CSV export creates a sharable file for tax documentation. Bring your own OpenAI key (billed directly to your account), or subscribe to Shelf-Snap Pro for managed access — no keys to add. Photos are sent to OpenAI for analysis and web-search snippets are sent to your chosen search provider only when you tap "Research price." Nothing else leaves your device.
