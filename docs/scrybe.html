How it works

Record. Transcribe. Transform.

Choose a mode, tap record

Pick Meeting, Idea, Tasks, Conversation, Story, Interview, or Journal — or create your own custom type. Recording runs as a foreground service with a persistent notification — configurable format, sample rate up to 48 kHz.

AI transcribes and identifies speakers

Audio goes to OpenAI Whisper (cloud) or on-device Whisper (tiny, base, or small model). Speaker diarization attributes the transcript to distinct voices, color-coded in the waveform and inline text.

Transform into structured output

Transformation profiles run your transcript through an LLM — clean up, summarize, extract action items, translate. Three built-in profiles, unlimited custom ones with a {{transcript}} template variable.

What's inside

record_voice_over

Speaker diarization

Audio is attributed to distinct speakers via an LLM pass. Each speaker gets a color in the waveform bars and inline transcript labels. Assign speakers to named person profiles.

task_alt

Task inbox

AI extracts action items with assignee and due date. A global task inbox aggregates tasks across all sessions with Today/Week/Mine filters. Manual task entry too.

segment

Batch transcription

Long recordings are chunked (≤16 min each, under Whisper's 25 MB limit). Each chunk saves immediately. If one fails, resume from where you left off — no re-upload.

smartphone

On-device AI

Whisper tiny/base/small for transcription (via Sherpa-ONNX). Gemma 2 2B for transforms, rename, clustering, and tag suggestions (via MediaPipe). No internet required.

search

AI semantic search

Search your recordings by meaning, not just keywords. OpenAI ranks recordings by relevance to a natural-language description. Plus keyword search, tag filters, and folder navigation.

auto_awesome

Smart Analyze

One tap runs title suggestion, tag suggestion, and recording-type classification in parallel. Accept or dismiss each individually. AI also clusters recordings into folders.

ios_share

Export everywhere

Markdown, plain text, or JSON. Obsidian vault export with YAML frontmatter. Share audio or transcript via the Android share sheet. Calendar integration for title suggestions.

insights

Insight visualizations

Sentiment timeline, topic markers (5–15 per session), and speech-density heatmap overlay on the playback waveform. Toggle on in Settings → AI Features.

Two ways to use AI

BYOK or Pro — your call

Scrybe never locks you in. Choose how you want AI to work, and change your mind anytime.

Bring your own key

Paste your OpenAI API key in Settings. Audio goes directly from your phone to OpenAI's API. You pay the provider at their published rates — typically pennies per recording.

Scrybe Pro — $1.99/mo

Subscribe via Google Play. AI calls route through the TwoBits Worker (api.twobits.app) — the OpenAI key stays server-side, never on your device. A monthly spend cap keeps usage in check.

Fully local

On-device Whisper for transcription, Gemma for transforms. Zero network calls. Download models once (150 MB – 2.6 GB), then everything runs on your phone's hardware.

About this app

Scrybe is a voice-to-document pipeline for Android. Record any conversation and get back a clean transcript, summary, action items, or whatever structure you need — shaped by the recording mode you choose. Seven built-in modes — Meeting, Idea, Tasks, Conversation, Story, Interview, Journal — each shape AI output differently. Recording types are first-class: create unlimited custom types with their own icon and system prompt. AI provider choice: OpenAI Whisper (cloud) or on-device Whisper tiny/base/small for transcription. OpenAI GPT models or on-device Gemma 2 2B for transforms. Swap providers anytime. Bring your own API key (billed directly to OpenAI) or subscribe to Scrybe Pro ($1.99/mo) for managed API access. Key capabilities: • Speaker diarization — color-coded waveform bars and inline transcript labels per speaker • Global task inbox — AI-extracted action items with assignee, due date, Today/Week/Mine filters • Batch transcription — long recordings chunked, each saved immediately, resume on failure • Smart Analyze — one tap for title, tags, and mode classification • AI semantic search — find recordings by meaning, not keywords • Insight visualizations — sentiment timeline, topic markers, speech density heatmap • Obsidian vault export — Markdown with YAML frontmatter • Folder organization — manual or AI-powered clustering • Multilingual transcription — preserves each spoken language rather than translating • Location tagging — optional GPS capture, reverse-geocoded city label
